A material's response to light reveals its electronic structure and underpins applications in photonics, sensing and energy conversion. This line computes that response **from first principles**.

The work covers **nonlinear optics**, in particular **second-harmonic generation (SHG)** in semiconductors and two-dimensional bilayers, as well as **band structures** and **linear optical spectra** of doped solids. It also includes the **electronic, magnetic and thermoelectric** properties of new compounds.

## Representative topics

- Nonlocality effects on SHG in semiconductors.
- SHG symmetry in moiré homobilayers.
- Unfolded band structure and optical properties of copper-doped KCl.
- Thermoelectric properties of Heusler alloys and nitrides.
