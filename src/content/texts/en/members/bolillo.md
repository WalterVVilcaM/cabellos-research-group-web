**Dr. Bolillo** is a full-time professor (PTC) at **Universidad Politécnica de Tapachula** and a member of the Cabellos Research Group. With a keen nose for energy minima, he explores nanocluster structure and stability within the research themes of Dr. José Luis Cabellos Quiroz.

## Education

- **PhD in Computational Chemistry:** low-energy gold and boron cluster structures using evolutionary algorithms and density functional theory (DFT).
- **MSc in Materials Science:** geometry, symmetry and stability of atomic aggregates.
- **BSc in Nanotechnology Engineering:** molecular modeling and electronic structure fundamentals.

## Research

He generates candidate structures, optimizes their geometries and compares isomers to identify low-energy configurations. He also examines how multicenter bonding stabilizes unconventional structures.

### Project: tracking the global minimum

His proposed computational atlas of gold nanoclusters connects relative energy, symmetry and bonding patterns. His laboratory rule: explore every corner of the energy surface before settling down for a nap.

## Teaching and group life

He teaches Molecular Modeling, Introduction to DFT and Global Optimization. He supports students through their first calculations and supervises active breaks with four-legged enthusiasm.
