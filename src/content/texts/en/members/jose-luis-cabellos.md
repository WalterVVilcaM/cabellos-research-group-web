Dr. José Luis Cabellos Quiroz is a research professor at the Universidad Politécnica de Tapachula, in the Research and Technological Development Office, and leads the Cabellos Research Group.

His work focuses on **computational chemistry and physics**: cluster structure prediction through global optimization, temperature effects on cluster properties, chemical bonding in unconventional species and the optical response of materials. He has published in journals such as *Angewandte Chemie*, *Physical Review B*, *Physical Review Materials*, *Chemical Communications* and *Inorganic Chemistry*.

He collaborates with research groups in Mexico, Latin America, China and India.
