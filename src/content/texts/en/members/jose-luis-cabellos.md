Dr. José Luis Cabellos Quiroz is a professor and researcher at the Universidad Politécnica de Tapachula, where he leads the Cabellos Research Group. He is a Level II member of Mexico's National System of Researchers (SNII) and a Category VI member of the Chiapas State System of Researchers (Honorary Scientific Researcher).

Born in Tepames, Colima, he studied Communications and Electronics Engineering at the Universidad de Colima and earned his Ph.D. in Science at the Centro de Investigaciones en Óptica (CIO) in León, Guanajuato, under the supervision of Dr. Bernardo Mendoza Santoyo. He did an academic stay at the Department of Physics of the University of Toronto and a postdoctoral stay at the Donostia International Physics Center (DIPC), Spain, under Prof. Ángel Rubio Secades.

His research focuses on **theoretical and computational chemistry, materials science, electronic structure, and linear and nonlinear optics** using first-principles methods. He has published in internationally recognized journals such as *Angewandte Chemie*, *Nanoscale*, *The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters*, *Chemical Communications*, *Nano Letters* and *Physical Review B*, with more than 115 scientific publications.

He has contributed to the development of computational tools for theoretical chemistry, including artificial intelligence algorithms, some of which are still used and further developed by other research groups, as well as computational models applied to optics and advanced materials.

He collaborates with researchers from Cinvestav, the University of Guadalajara, the University of Guanajuato, the Centro de Investigaciones en Óptica, the University of Arkansas and the Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter, among other national and international institutions. He has supervised and mentored students at the undergraduate, master's, doctoral and postdoctoral levels.
